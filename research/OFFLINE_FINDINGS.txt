Unresolved ownership — deliberate blocker, not an optional heuristic
- Descriptor flags mean local simulation authority, not the player who summoned.
  A host may own the simulation of multiple players' sentries.
- Health.lastCreditor (+0x38) means attacker of the sentry. It is NOT its summoner.
- HD2Runtime scripts/research_event_actions.py traces FireProjectile at 0x13A9700
  setting EDX=source entity and calling game+0x129C690 (credited-peer lookup).
  Its body and originating spawn/credit assignment must be decoded. The caller
  alone does not establish a safe native getter ABI or permanent summoner identity.
- No guessed offset, nearby-player heuristic or unverified native call is enabled.
  src/ownership.lua explicitly returns verified=false. The pure model filters
  unresolved/foreign owners. Packaged archive is labelled OFFLINE PROTOTYPE.
